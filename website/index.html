Questions schools ask

Is our school's data safe?

Each school's data is kept separate. Passwords are stored one-way, so nobody — not even support — can read them. Phone numbers and addresses stay hidden from support unless the principal allows access for 24 hours, and every change is recorded in an activity log.

Do parents need a smartphone?

Parents with an Android phone get everything in the app. One login covers all their children at the school.

Is the app in Hindi?

The app is in English today. This website is in Hindi and English, and Hindi in the app is planned.

What does it cost?

Schools pay per student per month for the sections they use, with a small minimum. Some schools are sponsored and use it free. Try the demo school first; the plan is set up when your school is added.

Can we move our existing records?

Yes. Download the Excel template from the app, fill in your classes, teachers and students, and upload it. The app checks every row before saving anything.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. School Hub is available for Android phones.
